02 / Current official rules

Total price has a defined meaning

The CARS Act addresses total-price disclosures in specified advertisements and first written communications, and material misrepresentations about availability at a communicated price. Total price includes dealer adjustments and items already installed, but excludes specified taxes, fees and charges; it is not automatically an all-in out-the-door amount.

We separate vehicle price, add-ons and other charges. The dated advertisement, vehicle identity, written negotiation and final line items provide the context.

02 / Current official rules

Document context comes before conclusions

CARS Act rules became operative October 1, 2026. They address price and add-on disclosures and certain used-vehicle cancellation transactions. Purchase date and transaction details matter; a checklist is not proof of a violation.

We distinguish signed amounts from quotes and written terms from reported conversations. Relevant official links accompany the issue flags.

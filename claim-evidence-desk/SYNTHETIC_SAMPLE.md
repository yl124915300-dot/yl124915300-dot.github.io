## 1. Scope and source-of-truth boundary

**Fictional case label:** DEMO-REFUND-001  
**Dispute type:** Unresolved partial refund on a returned online camera bundle  
**Customer's stated concern (unverified):** Seller acknowledged a return but only part of the original payment appears as credited.  
**Scope of review:** Index supplied events; reconcile reported charges and credits; identify conflicting or missing documentation.  
**Out of scope:** Decide who is legally entitled to money, interpret contract law, advise or represent a claimant, file bank/card or court documents, guarantee recovery, or contact either party.

### 1D. Missing-information checklist for a reviewer

- [ ] Confirm *which* order line items and taxes the merchant agreed to refund, if any; source: contemporaneous policy and transaction-level terms.
- [ ] Obtain definitive return acceptance record, if available, instead of treating the carrier scan as acceptance.
- [ ] Obtain merchant refund transaction IDs and their timestamps, if provided.
- [ ] Reconcile all credits, chargebacks, exchanges, gift-card offsets and payment-method changes before calculating the final balance.
- [ ] Confirm bank credit records using **masked** statements only; never share full card numbers, logins or identity documents.
- [ ] Resolve timezone differences and clearly label each source's local date.
- [ ] Record the exact documents reviewed and any contradictory dates or amounts, without altering originals.

## 2. Deliverable acceptance criteria

A client or supervising professional can check the factual output independently:

1. Every date and amount has a cited *supplied source ID*.
2. Reconciliation totals can be recomputed without guesswork.
3. Contradictions and unknowns are visible, rather than silently treated as facts.
4. No legal conclusions, entitlement judgments, or court/bank submission instructions appear.
5. Files are appropriately minimized and redacted; written authorization and secure handling requirements would be agreed **before** any real case data.

**No real orders, customer materials, payment transactions or legal filings were involved in producing this demonstration.**

**Current service status: Free self-service checklist only. Paid orders are closed. No legal advice or official settlement affiliation.**

The existing [Claim Evidence Desk site](https://claim-evidence-desk.naijunyu.chatgpt.site/) provides a **free, local-in-browser Kia window-regulator paperwork checklist**. ## For organizations assessing administrative overflow support

We are exploring a **narrowly scoped, non-legal clerical service** for consumer refund and small-claims record sets. The proposed output for one *properly authorized and redacted* case consists of:

1. A dated, source-linked **event timeline** with unresolved questions marked.
2. A **transaction/refund reconciliation** that distinguishes charges, credits and missing amounts without guessing.
3. A **document/exhibit index** tracing each factual statement to the supplied record.
4. A **missing-evidence checklist** and an acceptance/QC checklist for the organization's own review.

**See the complete [fictional demonstration packet](SYNTHETIC_SAMPLE.md)** — including example arithmetic and source-to-claim traceability. It contains no real consumer information.

02 / Current official rules

Optionality and benefit are separate questions

Rules operative October 1, 2026 address clear written optionality disclosures when a dealer makes written add-on representations during negotiations. They also prohibit charges for products that do not benefit the buyer, while allowing selected products that do.

What was represented, selected and covered are fact and document questions. We compare your file with official information; we do not declare every warranty, GAP product or dealer-installed item unlawful.

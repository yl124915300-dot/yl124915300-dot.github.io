01 — Slay the Spire 2 / 杀戮尖塔2

0 HP lost ≠ 0 resources lost

The player report: In CombatSolver #239, the player said a solver chose EndTurn despite having three attacks that, according to their later comment, could kill the Fat Gremlin. The encounter would end either way, but escape changes its gold reward.

Documented rule: The published STS2 v0.107.1 change log says that if the Gremlin Merc hadn't stolen any gold and Fat Gremlin escaped, the player now receives 50% of normal encounter gold rather than none. It does not mean that every escaping enemy halves rewards.

Why it matters: A line valued only by HP loss can wrongly treat kill and escape as equivalent. Gold recovered, escape versus kill, energy, and health should be considered separately. Example arithmetic: if a hypothetical normal reward were 40 gold, the documented escape condition awards 20. Forty gold is an illustration, not this player's observed reward.

Checklist for a fair comparison

  1. Check exact build, stolen-gold status, escape timer and remaining enemy HP.
  2. Check each alleged lethal attack's energy cost, target and effective damage.
  3. Compare kill and escape outcomes: HP lost, actual reward, stolen gold recovered, and execution legality.
  4. Reproduce any proposed alternative in the right environment before calling it a verified fix.

Limit: GamePilot did not run this game or reproduce the solver defect. The three-attack lethal claim is from the player. Our public diagnostic reply suggests tests, not a confirmed fix.

Your call: Should a solver prefer preserving HP or earning more gold when both lines end the encounter?

Sources: player's follow-up · Fat Gremlin mechanics · published v0.107.1 notes.

02 — Path of Exile 2 / 流放之路2

Entangle: does the slow buy you time to dodge?

Player's confusion: An official-forum report argues that Entangle's generalized “Slow” wording suggests attack and cast speed should change, yet only enemy movement was observed to slow.

Reference answer: The community mechanics page describes the current documented Entangle effect as movement-speed slow only. Data listings describe a 15% slow per attached vine, but do not by themselves establish attack/cast slowdown or the precise way multiple vines stack.

Practical decision: Treat this as movement control, not guaranteed reduction in attacks per second. If your build depends on avoiding hits, test rather than counting a generic tooltip as an attack-speed debuff.

A useful four-step reproduction

  1. Note the exact client patch, Entangle gem level, supports and monster type.
  2. Observe several attacks with no other slows active; record their timings.
  3. Apply vines, then separately measure movement and attack/cast intervals.
  4. Repeat and share anonymized timestamps, not a claim based on one animation.

Limit: The original report is from December 2025. GamePilot has not repeated this on the October 2026 build; patches may change mechanics.

Your call: Are you seeing movement-only slowdown in the current patch? Which build, monster and supports?

Sources: official player report · PoE2 wiki · PoE2DB.

03 — Balatro / 小丑牌

Blue Joker is not a fixed +104 Chips.

Documented ability: Blue Joker adds 2 Chips for every card remaining in the draw deck when it scores. It does not count your hand, played cards or discarded cards. The bonus falls as the deck is drawn down, so early-Blind value differs from late-Blind value.

Rule: Blue Joker Chips = 2 × cards still in draw deck. This is an additive Chips effect; multiplying that by Mult is only a simplified score estimate when other interactions are excluded.

Decision checklist

  1. Count what remains in your draw deck now, not the whole deck.
  2. Compare its +Chips contribution to a replacement Joker's actual effect at your current Mult; a +Mult or ×Mult Joker is not directly comparable by printed value alone.
  3. Recalculate after more drawing or discarding.
  4. Include synergy, card modifications, boss effects and the rest of the build before selling.

Limit: The published ability supports the formula. Without a player's hand, Jokers, upgrades and Blind, there is no defensible universal “sell it” recommendation.

Your call: How many cards were actually left in your deck when Blue Joker stopped helping?

Sources: ability from referenced game data · community reference.

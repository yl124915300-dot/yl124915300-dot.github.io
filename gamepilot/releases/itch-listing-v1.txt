GamePilot — Balatro Hand Coach
Short description: Compare ordinary-card Balatro plays, understand the score, and re-enter the next state. Manual input. Free.

ENGLISH PROJECT DESCRIPTION
A small, unofficial early-run practice tool for Balatro. Enter 1–8 unique ordinary cards and the current base Chips/Mult from your game's Run Info. GamePilot enumerates every 1–5-card selection, ranks supported immediate scores, and shows which cards actually score.
After playing, optionally record the observed score and manually enter the next visible state. This is not automatic game tracking.

TRY THE DEMO
Click "Run synthetic demo". AH KH QH JH TH 9C 9D 2S has 218 legal selections. The best supported selection is AH KH QH JH TH: (100 + 51) × 8 = 1,208. The cards and target are synthetic, not a player's screenshot or testimonial.
Then click "Enter next state", enter your own ordinary cards, check the current base hand values, confirm the supported rules, and compare again.

STRICT SCOPE
No Jokers, card enhancements, editions, seals, debuffs, Boss effects, Plasma Deck or mods. No duplicate card codes. No consumable actions, discard probabilities, economy or future-draw optimization.
An optional local image preview is only a visual reference for manual entry. Screenshot AI is BLOCKED: no image model, OCR, automatic state recognition or screenshot-to-advice pipeline is connected.
Immediate supported-score ranking is not a guarantee of the best long-term action or a won run.

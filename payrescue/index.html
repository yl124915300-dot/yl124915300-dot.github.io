PayRescue · WooCommerce + PayPal

Check whether paid PayPal orders are stuck in WooCommerce.

PayRescue runs a read-only 30-day audit for payment-state mismatches such as PayPal completed while WooCommerce still shows Pending or Failed.

What the audit checks

Paid but stuck
PayPal says completed; WooCommerce does not.
Pending payment
Orders that may need status verification.
Failed orders
Orders that may still have a valid PayPal payment.
Recoverable GMV
Only counted after PayPal status, amount and currency match.

Safety boundary

No card numbers or CVV are stored. Recovery is off by default. The audit does not automatically change WooCommerce order status.

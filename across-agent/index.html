Designed workflow

01 / QUOTE

Inspect route and costs

Determine available networks and token routes, exact recipient output, and the full proposed fee before any wallet action.

02 / AUTHORIZE

Wallet-owner signature

Non-custodial design. A wallet owner reviews and signs each authorized transaction. The agent never receives private keys.

03 / VERIFY

Track to settlement

Observe origin transaction, Across deposit status, destination fill, expiry/refund, and fee-recipient evidence.

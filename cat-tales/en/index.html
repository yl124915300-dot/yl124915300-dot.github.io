🎙️ Your privacy

No account, server-side recording or conversation storage. Browser speech services may process audio remotely. Optional model inference stays on-device.

🔎 What models can do

YAMNet labels possible sounds. EfficientDet-Lite0 detects cat/dog-shaped objects. Neither proves which cat made a sound nor understands cat intentions.

🐈 Your cat comes first

Never frighten or provoke your cat for an app demo. Trouble breathing, inability to urinate or collapse warrants urgent veterinary attention. Not medical advice.

Compare replacements in the right order

1. Establish the exact identity

Read the complete part number, identification number and revision from the physical label. Generic listing titles and shared base codes leave important questions unresolved.

2. Match the installed system

For motors, compare feedback, connectors, mounting, shaft and brake. For the MDS-CH-CV power supply, check the installed system, software version and input/output ratings against the Mitsubishi documentation linked in its guide.

3. Compare condition and return exposure

New, surplus, refurbished and Used labels are different claims. Read the tested-condition evidence, return window and who pays return freight. A repair-service price does not buy a replacement unit.

4. Use one delivery destination

Compare shipping, tax and dispatch for your actual postcode. A domestic ship-from location does not cancel a seller-away notice.

What these pages establish

They show a small, dated sample of publicly available asking prices and terms. They do not establish a universal market price, seller authorization, reserved stock or fitment for a particular machine. Follow the source links to check changes before purchasing.

Model-specific buying checks

What to verify before buying

Match the physical nameplate, not the listing title alone

Obtain a photo of the actual MDSKSBS056-33 nameplate and its complete Lenze identification number. The cheapest Used sample puts the exact number in its Model field but leaves MPN as Does Not Apply; that is not a confirmed nameplate match.

Source 1

Separate motor voltage from drive supply voltage

The catalogue separates motor rated voltage from mains supply voltage. Match the physical motor nameplate to the installed drive and machine documentation; a shared supply-voltage label does not establish a drop-in replacement or direct mains connection.

Source 1

Verify the accessory and mechanical variant

Lenze's ordering checklist distinguishes brake voltage, B14/B5 mounting, shaft with or without keyway, IP54/IP65 and plug versus terminal-box connection. Compare each against the removed unit and its drawing. Do not infer all of them from a shared MDSKS056 base number.

Source 1

Price the return risk and dispatch delay

The US$2,318.97 New sample does not accept seller returns; the US$2,569.02 New sample offers 30-day seller-paid returns; the US$904.99 Used sample offers 60-day buyer-paid returns. The New sellers show away notices through 8 and 10 October respectively. Recheck actual dispatch, destination terms and the motor's tested condition before selecting on price.

Keep repairs separate from replacement purchases

GES lists separate new/surplus, refurbished and repair prices. Its US$899 repair service is not an available replacement motor and is excluded from the listing price range.

What to check before buying

  1. Drive interface: these docks take standard SATA drives. M.2 NVMe SSDs do not fit these SATA bays.
  2. Clone direction: make sure the source and target are inserted in the labelled slots. Offline cloning overwrites data on the target. Back up important files first.
  3. Target capacity: use a destination drive with at least the source drive's actual capacity. Check any special SSD or encryption requirements in the manual.
  4. Freight and delivery: without the Australian delivery postcode there is no defensible final price or delivery estimate. Compare checkout totals and return policies before paying.
  5. Need an M.2 SSD? This comparison does not cover NVMe docks or mixed NVMe/SATA cloning devices.

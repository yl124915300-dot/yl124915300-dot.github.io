Shopify CSV import errors: what to fix first

If Shopify rejects your product CSV, diagnose the exact failure instead of guessing or allowing AI to rewrite the entire catalog.

Shopify's official troubleshooting page lists several distinct errors. These are examples of what to check—not assurances that a local CSV scan can see every server-side validation rule.

Common errors and what they mean

Import errorWhat to check
Invalid CSV headerUse Shopify's supported product CSV column names, with no extra spaces or nonstandard custom headers.
Illegal quoting on lineVerify straight double-quote escapes and UTF-8 encoding. A quoted cell may include a comma or newline.
Options are not uniqueReview duplicate combinations of option values under the same product handle, not just duplicate SKUs.
Price can't be blankVerify the variant-level price and any other required product data for your import scenario.
Could not download imageConfirm a publicly accessible absolute image URL and correct file/format. A local format check cannot test image hosting.

Three easy-to-miss mistakes

  1. Image-only rows: Shopify permits extra rows for product images; blank Title and price cells on these rows can be normal.
  2. Variant-dependent columns: Updating a SKU without corresponding Option1 name/value fields can replace existing variants. Review the variant deletion risk before importing.
  3. Compare-at pricing: A lower compare-at price than the active selling price may be intentional or an error. Review it; do not auto-correct without checking merchant policy.

What Catalog Check can and cannot do

It can parse a product CSV locally and flag suspicious missing fields, prices, repeated cross-product SKUs, option combinations, and obvious image URL format problems. It cannot tell whether your Shopify account will accept the file, whether your product images are hosted correctly, whether you will rank in ChatGPT, or whether your store is eligible for Business Agent.

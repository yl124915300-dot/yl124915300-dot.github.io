Two routes after an airline refusal

Aer Lingus cancelled one leg of a booking. If you booked on its website or app and want your money back, choose “Request Cash Refund” under “Aer Lingus cancelled my flight” on its cancellations page. If you still need another leg, confirm which flights the refund will cancel before submitting. For a third-party booking, contact the seller. If refused, ask for a written review and identify the flight the airline originally cancelled.

Disputed compensation after involuntary denied boarding on departure from Spain. Ask the airline in writing which distance it used and whether it reduced the amount for rerouting. Keep the boarding pass, your complaint and its offer. If the reply is unsatisfactory or a month passes without a reply, you can apply free of charge to Spain’s aviation authority, AESA. Apply within one year of your complaint to the airline. Submit documents only through the official channel.

Know which right you are using

Compensation

Potential fixed-rate EU261 payment in some disruptions, subject to flight coverage, arrival delay, notice, cause and exceptions.

Check potential eligibility →

Ticket refund

Reimbursement of eligible unused travel after certain cancellations or long departure delays. This is not the same as compensation.

Understand the difference →

Care expenses

Meals, hotels and transfers may be covered in qualifying disruptions. Keep reasonable receipts and follow the airline's claim process.

        Refund ≠ compensation ≠ care reimbursement

        Ticket reimbursement usually concerns unused transport after eligible cancellation or a long departure delay. Fixed statutory compensation is a separate right that depends on fault, delay at final destination, notice, and other exceptions. Expenses for reasonable care can have a separate procedure and supporting receipts.

        Regulation (EC) 261/2004 is used for the current V0 screening. The 2026 amendment 2026/2202 applies from 23 October 2027, not now. Other national laws may apply.

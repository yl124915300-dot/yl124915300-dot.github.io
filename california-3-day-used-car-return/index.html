02 / Current official rules

Why the date and disclosure matter

The CARS Act became operative October 1, 2026. Covered dealer retail used-vehicle sales or leases of $50,000 or less can have a three-day cancellation right. The period generally starts the next calendar day; dealership closures, mileage, condition, return requirements, fees and exceptions matter.

DMV summaries are not fully consistent: its Bill of Rights page mixes new three-day wording with older option-agreement information. The current statute prohibits charging for the cancellation right itself, while permitted restocking fees may apply. We flag the difference rather than assume every buyer has the same right.

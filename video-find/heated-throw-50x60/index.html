Safety before any checkout

Safety notice: In July 2026 the US CPSC specifically warned against Melinora electric heated blankets because of burn/fire hazards. Those products are not offered here. Before using any electric blanket, inspect the cord and controller, review exact-model recall status and follow the maker's instructions—especially about folding, covering, laundering and overnight use.

Read the CPSC warning and pre-use inspection guidance. This is a shopping comparison, not a safety certification or medical recommendation.

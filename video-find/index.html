STEP 02 / FRAME

Pick the object

Awaiting frame
Drop or paste a screenshot, freeze a video frame, or try the illustrated sample.

Draw a rectangle on the frame to select the exact item. On a supported object-detection result, tap its label to choose that object.

Optional label reading: activate after selecting a frame region. Text is only a suggestion, never an automatically verified brand or model.

When you choose label reading, FrameFind downloads a pinned open-source OCR runtime and English language data into your browser. The cropped image is analyzed on your device, not uploaded to an OCR server. It may fail offline or misread logos and model numbers.

Our existing locally hosted EfficientDet-Lite0 model recognizes broad objects such as backpacks, bags, bottles, chairs and laptops, not exact brands or SKUs. Frames are processed in your browser. For difficult fashion items, save the selected object crop and optionally upload it at Google Lens for a separate visual-similarity search. We do not upload your crop to Lens.

Frequently asked questions

Can FrameFind automatically read frames from any TikTok or YouTube link?

No. The original platforms may block direct access, canvas capture or embedding. If the file is directly accessible with suitable browser permissions, you can select a time on this page. Otherwise, the user explicitly shares a video tab or supplies a screenshot. We do not circumvent access restrictions.

Does the AI recognize the exact product model?

Not reliably yet. EfficientDet provides category and bounding-box suggestions. The shopper can add a visible brand, model, color and other clues. The resulting retailer links are searches, not verified visual-identical SKUs or live price comparisons.

Is the product search free?

Yes, the current beta is free. No account, payment, subscription or recording of your uploaded images is required. A future high-usage paid plan would be announced separately and would require functioning server-side billing.

Can I order here? Who gets paid?

We do not sell goods, collect card details, or take orders. Merchants handle payment and delivery on their own sites. No general retailer affiliate agreement, commission tracking or subscription payment is represented as active on this page.

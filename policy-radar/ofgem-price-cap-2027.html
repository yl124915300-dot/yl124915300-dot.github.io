Ofgem price cap: next announcement due by 25 November 2026

Ofgem has scheduled its next domestic energy price cap announcement by 25 November 2026, covering 1 January–31 March 2027. This is a planned publication date, not a grant or a guaranteed reduction in bills.

Official schedule checked 17 November 2026

Jurisdiction: England, Scotland and Wales; not Northern Ireland.

This is independent information, not a government service or a personal eligibility determination.

Who is affected?

The energy price cap regulates maximum unit rates and standing charges for default tariffs in England, Scotland and Wales. It is not a fixed upper limit on an individual's total bill. Fixed-rate deals may behave differently; Northern Ireland has separate energy regulation.

What happens next?

Once Ofgem publishes the January–March 2027 rates, compare the unit price and daily standing charge relevant to your region, meter type and payment method. The announcement can come before the scheduled date.

What should you do?

Use your bill or supplier account to find the current tariff, actual electricity/gas use and payment method. Do not assume the headline 'typical household' estimate equals your bill. If struggling to pay, contact your energy supplier for support.

England · Local authority benefits

Find local emergency support, without paying an intermediary.

England's Crisis and Resilience Fund is managed by local councils. Rules, availability and application routes depend on where you live. Enter a postcode to find your council, or open one of the verified council guides below.

Find the council for your postcode

Typing a postcode alone sends nothing. If you choose “Find my council”, your postcode is sent directly from this browser to Postcodes.io, an independent UK postcode lookup service. Policy Radar does not receive, record or retain it. You can also browse councils below without submitting a postcode.

Postcode lookup is optional and not an eligibility test. Do not enter your full address, name or benefit account number.

Direct links to four verified councils

The selected councils have published public guidance and routes to request emergency support. Current funding availability can change; always use their official sites.

Birmingham

For residents experiencing financial hardship, particularly with food and energy costs. The council says previous recipients may face a 12-month wait before another award.

The council reports it is currently accepting expressions of interest; it can pause enquiries when waiting lists are full. No grant is guaranteed. The council warns that no payment is required to apply.

Manchester

For residents on a low income facing a sudden financial shock and unable to meet basic living costs such as food or energy.

Manchester publishes separate help for emergency food, heating, essential household goods and certain travel costs. Housing support has its own route.

Leeds

For residents on low incomes facing hardship, emergencies or unexpected essential expenses.

Leeds links separate Crisis Payment and Housing Payment procedures, as well as free financial resilience advice. Check the current council page before applying.

Bristol

For Bristol residents on low incomes facing unexpected essential costs. Different routes exist for crisis support and housing payments.

The council describes distinct support for people leaving care, some families and eligible residents facing urgent financial problems. The scheme is not a guaranteed payment.

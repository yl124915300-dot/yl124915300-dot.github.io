Warm Home Discount 2026/27: £150 eligibility and 26 February 2027 deadline

The Warm Home Discount is a one-off £150 discount on eligible households' electricity bills, not a cash payment. Most eligible households in England and Wales receive it automatically.

Official guidance checked 17 November 2026

Jurisdiction: England and Wales only; Scotland has different steps; not available in Northern Ireland.

This is independent information, not a government service or a personal eligibility determination.

Who may qualify?

For the 2026/27 scheme, check whether you or your partner received qualifying means-tested benefits on 23 August 2026, whether your electricity supplier participates, and whether you or your partner were named on the electricity bill. Individual eligibility must be checked through GOV.UK.

What dates matter?

Letters are expected from late October 2026 to early January 2027. If you think you should qualify but do not receive a letter by early January, contact the scheme. If you are asked to supply missing information, contact the helpline no later than 26 February 2027. If eligible, the supplier should apply the bill discount by 31 March 2027.

What should you do?

Read the official eligibility page and any letter you receive. If the letter says your discount is automatic, you do not need to pay a third party or submit a new application. Never share bank details with someone claiming to secure the discount.

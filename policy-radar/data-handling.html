How this preview handles data

Policy Radar UK shows public government information. It is not a government service, benefits application or legal advice provider. This page explains the data flows in the currently deployed preview.

No account or mailing list

We do not currently provide an email sign-up, user accounts, payment collection or personalised server-side reminders. RSS and calendar subscriptions are managed by the software that you choose; our website does not obtain a subscriber roster.

We have not installed a third-party visitor analytics beacon or advertising tracker on these public pages. We therefore cannot report a verified count of individual visitors or subscribers.

Preferences are stored locally on your device

If you select Save on this device, your UK nation, chosen topics and optional user-entered childcare reconfirmation date are stored in your browser's local storage. Your previous approved-notice review baseline is also stored in the same browser if you explicitly start tracking. This data is not uploaded by the site's JavaScript.

Use the page's “Clear” controls or your browser settings to erase those saved items. People who use the same browser profile could see them.

Benefit-checker answers stay in this browser session

The Warm Home Discount action checker uses your answers to choose a next-action message in your browser. It does not ask for names, account numbers, addresses or proof documents and does not submit your answers to our own server. It checks a public JSON file to determine whether relevant official guidance monitoring is current.

Its result is a general aid, not a legal or eligibility decision; confirm all important decisions with the government scheme.

Optional postcode lookup uses a separate service

On the local-support finder, a postcode is not sent merely by typing it. If you press “Find my council”, your browser sends the postcode directly to the third-party Postcodes.io API and receives a local authority name. Policy Radar does not separately store your postcode, but the provider may process technical request information under its own terms.

You can avoid this lookup and browse government links without entering a postcode.

Hosting, public feedback and outgoing links

  • Pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection data, including network logs, according to its own policies. We do not directly receive a GitHub Pages visitor analytics dashboard through this site.
  • Optional policy requests and correction reports open public GitHub Issues. A GitHub account is required. Do not post names, full postcodes, benefits details, financial information or other sensitive data in public issues.
  • When you open GOV.UK, council, Ofgem or other external services, those sites handle that visit under their own privacy practices.
  • This is a functional preview, not a complete legal privacy notice for any future paid, analytics or email operation. Those services must be assessed and disclosed before activation.

Cookies and other storage

Our preview does not set analytics or advertising cookies. Browser local storage is used only after a visitor explicitly saves preferences or starts a policy review baseline. UK PECR now has a limited statistical-purposes exception for appropriately aggregated analytics used only to improve the service; it does not cover identifying or profiling individual visitors or advertising measurement. UK GDPR duties still apply. Before adding any analytics, we must document the actual provider, data collected, lawful basis, aggregation, retention and whether consent is required for that particular configuration.

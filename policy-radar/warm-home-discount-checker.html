What to check

No sign-up · No payment · No data collection
The Warm Home Discount is a one-off £150 bill discount, not a bank transfer. For England and Wales, the 2026/27 qualifying date was 23 August 2026. The majority of eligible households receive their discount automatically. Official GOV.UK conditions ↗

For England/Wales 2026/27: Universal Credit, Housing Benefit, income-related ESA or Pension Credit. If you only had another benefit, don't assume it qualifies.

Check the official supplier list ↗. Supplier eligibility and your circumstances need official verification.

The person receiving a qualifying benefit (or their partner, as recorded for benefits) generally needs to be the electricity account holder/named on the bill.

Park homes without a direct supplier relationship can have a separate grant scheme.

Important confirmed dates

23 August 2026: England/Wales qualification snapshot (already passed).

Late October 2026–early January 2027: eligible or potentially eligible households are sent a letter.

Early January 2027: if you expect a letter but haven't received one, contact the Warm Home Discount scheme.

26 February 2027: deadline to contact the helpline if the letter requests further information.

31 March 2027: supplier should apply the discount for qualifying households by this date.

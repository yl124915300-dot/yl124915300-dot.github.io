Terms

Updated 29 September 2026. Intent Commerce is a connector candidate and is not yet available to public Muse buyers.

What the connector does

Intent Commerce compares a buyer's stated shopping request with supplier records, shows at most three ranked offers, and provides a supplier checkout link only after the buyer chooses one. It does not accept payment inside Muse, place an order, book a time slot, or promise that an offer remains available.

Offer and checkout limits

Supplier listings may be incomplete or change. Groupon local voucher listings do not confirm a same-night reservation or redemption slot; any shown distance is an approximate straight-line measure. The supplier sets the final price, availability, checkout, payment, fulfillment, refunds and its own terms. Check those details before deciding to buy.

Affiliate links

Some eligible supplier links may earn Intent Commerce a commission. Commission does not affect offer ranking. A checkout link does not prove attribution, a purchase or a commission. Groupon tracking is not currently verified.

Acceptable use and changes

Use the connector only for lawful shopping requests. Do not send payment card information, passwords or other sensitive account credentials in a shopping request. The service may limit requests and may be unavailable during supplier or index refreshes. These terms and the service may be updated; the date above identifies this version.

Intent Commerce

Turn an existing shopping request into a short, ranked comparison, then let the buyer choose whether to continue at the supplier's checkout.

How it works

  1. Parse the buyer's request, including budget, location and stated constraints.
  2. Compare available supplier records from Groupon Partner Storefront and Shopify Global Catalog. Show up to three matching offers with prices, reasons and limitations.
  3. Only after the buyer chooses an offer, provide the supplier's checkout link. Payment and fulfillment happen with the supplier.

Current limits

  • Groupon inventory is US only and local deal coverage may be partial.
  • A listed voucher does not confirm a same-night reservation or redemption slot. Distance, when shown, is approximate straight-line distance.
  • Prices and availability should be checked at supplier checkout. This connector does not place orders, collect payment details, or confirm purchases.
  • This connector has not been approved for the Muse Directory. Public buyer access is not available yet.

Affiliate disclosure

Some eligible supplier links may earn a commission. Commission does not affect ranking. Groupon commission tracking is not currently verified, and a checkout link alone does not prove a sale or commission.

Compare oat milk multipacks by actual price per litre

A six-pack of one-litre cartons and six 946 mL cartons can look equivalent in a price list. A minimum order, delivery fee or substitution can erase a lower unit price.

What to check before you pay

  1. Match the product. Enter the exact shelf name or printed package barcode. Barista, unsweetened and ordinary variants should stay separate. Check whether the barcode identifies a single carton or the whole case.
  2. Use a common unit. Divide the total attributable cost by the total litres you must buy. Six 946 mL cartons contain 5.676 L; six one-litre cartons contain 6 L. Keep a minimum order of two cases in the calculation.
  3. Check the observation. Compare the date, currency and store on the shelf record. Check the current seller basket for delivery, minimums, and substitution rules. Do not turn a dated receipt into a quote.

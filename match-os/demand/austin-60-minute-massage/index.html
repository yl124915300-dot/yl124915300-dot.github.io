Find a 60-minute solo massage in Austin without relying on “from” prices

Discount catalog cards often show the lowest option. It might cover 30 minutes, a different location or a weekday-only voucher, even when the title mentions 60 minutes.

What to check before you pay

  1. Match the option. Open the offer menu and select 60 minutes for one person. Check whether the time means hands-on treatment or includes intake and changing. Do not compare a couple’s package with a solo price.
  2. Check the actual appointment. Confirm the Austin location and available slot before buying a voucher. Check first-visit limits, blackout dates, booking lead time, and whether the voucher expires before your visit.
  3. Get the complete price. Check taxes, mandatory charges and cancellation rules in the booking flow. A starting price is a lead; it does not establish the full cost or book a slot.

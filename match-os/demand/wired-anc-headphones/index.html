Headphones that keep noise cancellation on with a 3.5 mm cable

A listing can say “wired and wireless” and “noise cancelling” without saying whether both work at once. This matters on a plane seat jack or a work computer where Bluetooth is disabled.

What to check before you pay

  1. Exact connection. Find the manufacturer manual for the exact model number. Check 3.5 mm analogue audio; USB-C charging by itself does not indicate USB-C audio.
  2. Active cancellation on cable. Look for an explicit wired-mode ANC statement and whether the headset has to be switched on. Treat passive sound isolation and Bluetooth-only ANC as separate features.
  3. What you actually pay. Match model, condition and region; compare the included cable, shipping, tax and return window. Ask whether the mic or controls work in wired mode if you need them.
  4. Sony WH-1000XM4 (manual-verified). Sony explicitly permits noise cancelling through its supplied 3.5 mm headphone cable while the headset is powered on. If powered off, wired audio can still work but ANC cannot. This is evidence for the WH-1000XM4 model only.
  5. soundcore Space Q45 (FAQ-verified). soundcore says the Space Q45 can activate noise cancelling by pressing the NC button after its AUX cable is connected. This confirms the wired ANC behavior for the Q45, not a current price, bundled cable, stock or delivery promise.
